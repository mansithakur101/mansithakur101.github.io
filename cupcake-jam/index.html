Cupcake Jam

A free puzzle game for Android

Fill the bakery's orders by tapping cupcakes out of the display case and into the matching boxes. Plan each move: only cupcakes with a clear path to the top can be picked, and there's just a small tray for the ones that don't fit yet. Earn stars to decorate your bakery, one cozy room at a time.

Support

Questions, a bug, or a purchase that didn't arrive? In the game, open Settings → Contact support: it starts an email with your game version and player ID already filled in, which helps us find your account. You can also write to honestpixelsupport@gmail.com.
